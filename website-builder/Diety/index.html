About Our Temple

Sacred Heritage

Meletheruvu Sree Mutharamman Temple stands as a beacon of spiritual devotion and cultural heritage in the heart of Swadeshabhimani Nagar, Neyyattinkara. This sacred abode is dedicated to the powerful goddess Sri Mutharamman, who is revered as the divine protector and benevolent mother.

Spiritual Significance

The temple serves as a spiritual sanctuary where devotees from far and wide come to seek the blessings of Sri Mutharamman. Known for her protective powers and ability to fulfill the wishes of her devotees, the goddess is particularly revered by families seeking prosperity, health, and happiness.

Community Center

Beyond its religious significance, the temple plays a vital role as a community center, bringing together people from all walks of life during festivals, ceremonies, and cultural events. The temple's annual celebrations showcase the rich traditions and cultural heritage of Kerala.

Our Deities

The divine trinity that blesses our sacred temple

Sri Mutharamman

Principal Deity

The powerful and benevolent goddess who protects her devotees and grants their wishes. Sri Mutharamman is revered as the divine mother who showers her blessings upon all who seek her with pure hearts.

Divine Protection Wish Fulfillment Maternal Blessings

Sri Parameswaran (Bhairavan)

Guardian Deity

The fierce guardian deity who protects the temple and its devotees from negative forces. Bhairavan is known for his power to remove obstacles and provide spiritual strength.

Protection Obstacle Removal Spiritual Strength

Bhadrakali

Divine Shakti

The fierce form of Goddess Durga, Bhadrakali represents divine feminine power and courage. She is worshipped for protection against evil and for empowerment in times of need.

Divine Power Courage Evil Protection
